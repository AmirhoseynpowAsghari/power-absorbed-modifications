Vertices (Sec. 3). Ultrasound, spin-independent coupling of strength Bq, η=+1: |B|2=|Bq|212(1+ss′cosΔθ), Δθ=θ𝐤−θ𝐤′. Electromagnetic absorption at q→0, η=−1: B=𝐀·𝐕ss′(𝐤) with 𝐕ss=∇𝐤ε𝐤s and an interband part Vs,−sx=2iVSOscoskxsinky/R𝐤, Vs,−sy=−2iVSOscoskysinkx/R𝐤, R𝐤=sin2kx+sin2ky.

1. Setting and conventions

The non-interacting part is H0(𝐤)=ε𝐤−μ+HSO(𝐤) with ε𝐤=−2t(coskx+cosky) and the Rashba term of the paper (HHM Eq. 14),

HSO(𝐤)=−2VSO(sinkyσx−sinkxσy)=−2VSOR𝐤𝐠^𝐤·σ,𝐠^𝐤=(cosθ𝐤,sinθ𝐤)
(1)

Its eigenstates are the helicity states (HHM Eqs. 15–17), eiθ𝐤=(sinky−isinkx)/R𝐤:

|𝐤s⟩=12(1,seiθ𝐤)T,ε𝐤s=ε𝐤−2sVSOR𝐤,s=±1
(2)

Time reversal T=iσyK keeps the helicity and maps 𝐤→−𝐤; since eiθ−𝐤=−eiθ𝐤,

T|𝐤s⟩=ρ𝐤s|−𝐤,s⟩,ρ𝐤s=se−iθ𝐤,ρ−𝐤s=−ρ𝐤s,T2=−1
(3)

(HHM write the time-reversed partner of c𝐤s as −seiθc−𝐤s: the same up to the overall sign convention of T.) The gap. Pairing is intraband, between |𝐤s⟩ and its time-reverse, and HHM (text after Eq. 22) find

Δ𝐤s=se−iθ𝐤Δ¯𝐤s=ρ𝐤sΔ¯𝐤s,Δ¯𝐤s=Δ0+Δss𝐤+Δx−ysR𝐤=Δ0−Δs4tε𝐤s(real)
(4)

In spin space this is a singlet Δ0+Δss𝐤 plus a triplet with 𝐝𝐤=Δx−y(sinky,−sinkx,0) along the Rashba vector, Δx−y=VSOΔs/2t (HHM Eq. 35). I checked numerically that this spin-space gap matrix projects onto exactly the form (4), diagonal in s (Sec. 5). Bogoliubov transformation (HHM Eq. 23), for the helicity operators a𝐤s (a𝐤s† creates |𝐤s⟩):

a𝐤s=u𝐤sγ𝐤s−ρ𝐤sv𝐤sγ−𝐤s†,u𝐤sv𝐤s=Δ¯𝐤s2E𝐤s,u𝐤s2−v𝐤s2=ξ𝐤sE𝐤s
(5)

u,v are real and even in 𝐤; the oddness of the gap sits in ρ𝐤s. In HHM's convention the pairing term is −12Δc†c† and v has the opposite overall sign; only products vv′ enter below.

2. Derivation

The perturbation is a one-body operator, the paper's H′=∑B(𝐤α|𝐤′α′)c𝐤α†c𝐤′α′; in the helicity basis

H′=∑𝐤𝐤′∑ss′B(𝐤s|𝐤′s′)a𝐤s†a𝐤′s′,B(𝐤s|𝐤′s′)=⟨𝐤s|O^|𝐤′s′⟩
(6)
Step 1 — insert (5).
a𝐤s†a𝐤′s′=uu′γ𝐤s†γ𝐤′s′+ρ𝐤s*ρ𝐤′s′vv′γ−𝐤sγ−𝐤′s′†−uρ𝐤′s′v′γ𝐤s†γ−𝐤′s′†−ρ𝐤s*vu′γ−𝐤sγ𝐤′s′
(7)
Step 2 — the scattering amplitude.

The second term of (7) is −ρ𝐤s*ρ𝐤′s′vv′γ−𝐤′s′†γ−𝐤s plus a constant. Relabelling (𝐤,s,𝐤′,s′)→(−𝐤′,s′,−𝐤,s), using ρ−𝐤s=−ρ𝐤s and the evenness of v, the coefficient of γ𝐤s†γ𝐤′s′ is

M(𝐤s|𝐤′s′)=B(𝐤s|𝐤′s′)u𝐤su𝐤′s′−ρ𝐤sρ𝐤′s′*v𝐤sv𝐤′s′B(−𝐤′s′|−𝐤s)
(8)

This is the paper's general expression M=Bukuk′−vkvk′∑ρρB(−k′α′|−kα) with the spin phases replaced by the helicity phases.

Step 3 — time-reversal lemma.

Let O^ be Hermitian with TO^T−1=ηO^. For an antiunitary T, ⟨Tϕ|O^|Tψ⟩=η⟨ψ|O^|ϕ⟩. With |−𝐤s⟩=ρ𝐤s*T|𝐤s⟩ from (3) this gives

B(−𝐤′s′|−𝐤s)=ηρ𝐤′s′ρ𝐤s*B(𝐤s|𝐤′s′)
(9)

This is the paper's relation ∑ρρB(−k′α′|−kα)=ηB(kα|k′α′), again with helicity phases: η=+1 for a scalar (density-like) operator, η=−1 for a current-like one.

Step 4 — the phases cancel.

Insert (9) into (8): the phase factor is ρ𝐤sρ𝐤′s′*·ρ𝐤′s′ρ𝐤s*=1, so the second term of (8) is −ηBv𝐤sv𝐤′s′ and (M) follows. This works because the gap carries exactly the phase ρ𝐤s (4), which makes v real. Had the phase of the gap been dropped, or |Δ𝐤s| used, a factor like ei(θ𝐤−θ𝐤′) would survive in the coherence term.

Step 5 — square it.

With u2=12(1+ξ/E), v2=12(1−ξ/E) and uv=Δ¯/2E from (5):

(uu′−ηvv′)2=u2u′2+v2v′2−2ηuu′vv′=12[1+ξξ′EE′−ηΔ¯Δ¯′EE′]
(10)

which is (C). Because Δ¯ changes sign at the node ε0=4tΔ0/Δs, the product Δ¯Δ¯′ must keep its sign.

Step 6 — pair creation.

The two cross terms of (7) create or annihilate pairs of quasiparticles. Antisymmetrising the creation term and using ⟨ψ|O^|Tϕ⟩=−η⟨ϕ|O^|Tψ⟩ (from the lemma and T2=−1) gives the amplitude Nab=Xab(uavb+ηubva) for γa†γb†, Xab=⟨a|O^|Tb⟩, and |N|2 as in (N).

3. The helicity vertex B(ks|k's')

3.1 Ultrasound: spin-independent coupling, η = +1

A deformation-potential coupling O^=Bqρ𝐪+h.c. is the identity in spin space, so B(𝐤s|𝐤′s′)=Bq⟨𝐤s|𝐤′s′⟩ with 𝐤=𝐤′+𝐪, and from (2)

⟨𝐤s|𝐤′s′⟩=12(1+ss′ei(θ𝐤′−θ𝐤)),|⟨𝐤s|𝐤′s′⟩|2=12(1+ss′cosΔθ),cosΔθ=𝐠^𝐤·𝐠^𝐤′
(11)

Together with (C), the complete ultrasonic matrix element for scattering is

|M(𝐤s|𝐤′s′)|2=|Bq|24(1+ss′cosΔθ)[1+ξξ′EE′−Δ¯Δ¯′EE′]
(12)

For pair creation (𝐤+𝐤′=𝐪) the overlap is with |−𝐤′s′⟩, and since θ−𝐤=θ𝐤+π the roles of intra- and inter-helicity are exchanged: |X|2=|Bq|212(1−ss′cosΔθ).

Selection rule. For q≪π/a the Rashba vectors at 𝐤 and 𝐤′=𝐤−𝐪 are nearly parallel: the weight is ≈1 for s′=s and ≈0 for s′=−s. The Brillouin-zone average of the two weights (independent of VSO, since only θ𝐤 enters), for 𝐪 along kx, is

3.2 Electromagnetic absorption: current vertex, η = −1

In the dipole limit (q→0) the coupling is 𝐀·𝐕^ with the velocity operator 𝐕=∂𝐤H0 (all constants absorbed in 𝐀). It is odd under T and diagonal in 𝐤:

Vx=2tsinkx+2VSOcoskxσy,Vy=2tsinky−2VSOcoskyσx
(13)

With (2), ⟨𝐤s|σx|𝐤s′⟩=scosθ𝐤 and ⟨𝐤s|σy|𝐤s′⟩=ssinθ𝐤 for s′=s, and −issinθ𝐤, iscosθ𝐤 for s′=−s; with cosθ𝐤=sinky/R𝐤, sinθ𝐤=−sinkx/R𝐤 this gives

Vssx=2tsinkx−2sVSOsinkxcoskxR𝐤=∂kxε𝐤s,Vs,−sx=2iVSOscoskxsinkyR𝐤
(14)
Vssy=2tsinky−2sVSOsinkycoskyR𝐤=∂kyε𝐤s,Vs,−sy=−2iVSOscoskysinkxR𝐤
(15)

The interband element equals (ε𝐤,−s−ε𝐤s)⟨𝐤s|∂𝐤|𝐤,−s⟩ with ε𝐤,−s−ε𝐤s=4sVSOR𝐤. From (M) and (N) with η=−1, 𝐤′=𝐤:

M(𝐤s|𝐤s′)=𝐀·𝐕ss′(u𝐤su𝐤s′+v𝐤sv𝐤s′),N(𝐤s,−𝐤s′)=𝐀·𝐕ss′(u𝐤sv𝐤s′−u𝐤s′v𝐤s)
(16)
  • Intraband (s′=s): M=𝐀·∇ε𝐤s (since u2+v2=1), but there is no energy transfer, so no absorption at ω≠0; and N(𝐤s,−𝐤s)≡0 — no pair breaking within one band at q=0 (BdG check: exactly zero).
  • Interband (s′=−s): scattering when E𝐤,−s−E𝐤s=ℏω, and pair creation above E𝐤++E𝐤−. Both are generically non-zero and are driven by VSO through the interband vertex.

So at q=0 the clean Rashba superconductor absorbs only through interband channels, which a constant-B treatment cannot represent. The paper's double integral over independent 𝐤,𝐤′ corresponds instead to momentum-non-conserving scattering, where the weights of Sec. 3.1 apply.

4. How this differs from the formulas in the paper

  1. Constant B. The paper averages B over angles and spin indices and takes it constant. In helicity space this replaces |⟨𝐤s|𝐤′s′⟩|2 by its uncorrelated-angle average 12 in all four channels. That is reasonable only if 𝐤 and 𝐤′ are uncorrelated; for small q it misplaces all the weight of the s′=−s channels (table above, Fig. 1b).
  2. Sign of the η term. With M=B(uu′−ηvv′), (10) gives −ηΔ¯Δ¯′/EE′. The paper's intermediate result is the η=+1 case, 12(1+ξξ′/EE′−ΔΔ′/EE′), but its final expressions for W and W/WN, as I read them in the extracted text, carry +ηΔΔ′/EE′; with η=+1 for ultrasound that exchanges the coherence factors of the two cases. Please check this against the typeset PDF.
  3. Signed gap. Δ¯𝐤s changes sign at ε0=4tΔ0/Δs=0.879t in the example below. In Δ¯Δ¯′ the sign must be kept; |Δ𝐤s| is wrong for transitions across the node.
  4. Sign of ξ in energy variables. The paper writes ε𝐤s−μ=+E2−Δ2 in the coherence factor. At a given E there are two roots ε±(E) (previous note) and ξ can have either sign, so
Cη(E,σ;E′,σ′)=12[1+ξσ(E)ξσ′(E′)EE′−ηΔσ(E)Δσ′(E′)EE′],ξσ(E)=εσ(E)−μ
(17)

with εσ(E), Δσ(E) and the band test of the Δ(E) note. Example (VSO=0.5t, Δ0=0.29255t, Δs=1.33119t, μ=3.72649t, W=4.2426t), pairs of states with E≈1.10t and E′≈2.00t. At E the roots are ε+=4.044t (ξ>0) and ε−=2.841t (ξ<0); at E′ they are ε−=1.747t and ε+=5.138t>W, which the band test discards. 5. Exact numerical check

The check does not use any helicity argument. I build the 4×4 Bogoliubov–de Gennes matrix in spin space, in the Nambu basis (c𝐤↑,c𝐤↓,c−𝐤↑†,c−𝐤↓†), with the physical gap matrix (singlet + triplet along the Rashba vector):

HBdG(𝐤)=(h(𝐤)D(𝐤)D†(𝐤)−hT(−𝐤)),D(𝐤)=[Δ0+Δss𝐤+Δx−yR𝐤𝐠^𝐤·σ]iσy
(18)

I diagonalise it numerically and read off the coefficients of γ†γ and γ†γ† in ∑Bc†c from the Nambu form diag(B𝐤,𝐤′,−B−𝐤′,−𝐤T). These are compared with the helicity-space formulas for random (𝐤,𝐤′,s,s′), with the scalar vertex and with the current vertex (two field directions).

6. Minimal code and files

import numpy as np

def spinor(k, s):                           # |ks> = (1, s e^{i theta})/sqrt2, HHM Eqs. 16-17
    R = np.hypot(np.sin(k[0]), np.sin(k[1]))
    return np.array([1.0, s*(np.sin(k[1]) - 1j*np.sin(k[0]))/R]) / np.sqrt(2)

def uv(k, s, D0, Ds, mu, VSO, t=1.0):       # u, v (v has the sign of Dbar), E, xi, Dbar
    eps = -2*t*(np.cos(k[0]) + np.cos(k[1])) - 2*s*VSO*np.hypot(np.sin(k[0]), np.sin(k[1]))
    xi, Db = eps - mu, D0 - Ds*eps/(4*t)    # Dbar_ks = D0 - Ds eps_ks / 4t  (signed)
    E = np.hypot(xi, Db)
    return np.sqrt((1 + xi/E)/2), np.sign(Db)*np.sqrt((1 - xi/E)/2), E, xi, Db

def M_helicity(k, s, kp, sp, vertex, eta, D0, Ds, mu, VSO, t=1.0):
    """M(ks|k's') = <ks|O|k's'> (u u' - eta v v').
    vertex(k, kp): 2x2 spin matrix of O;  eta = +1 (T-even) or -1 (T-odd)."""
    u,  v  = uv(k,  s,  D0, Ds, mu, VSO, t)[:2]
    up, vp = uv(kp, sp, D0, Ds, mu, VSO, t)[:2]
    B = np.vdot(spinor(k, s), vertex(k, kp) @ spinor(kp, sp))
    return B*(u*up - eta*v*vp)

def vertex_scalar(k, kp):                   # ultrasound: spin independent, eta = +1
    return np.eye(2)

def vertex_current(k, kp, A, VSO, t=1.0):   # EM, q -> 0: A . dH/dk at (k+k')/2, eta = -1
    km = ((k[0] + kp[0])/2, (k[1] + kp[1])/2)
    sx, sy = np.array([[0, 1], [1, 0]]), np.array([[0, -1j], [1j, 0]])
    Vx = 2*t*np.sin(km[0])*np.eye(2) + 2*VSO*np.cos(km[0])*sy
    Vy = 2*t*np.sin(km[1])*np.eye(2) - 2*VSO*np.cos(km[1])*sx
    return A[0]*Vx + A[1]*Vy
